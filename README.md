An out-of-office auto-reply with a resignation letter hiding inside it.
The agent has quit inbox duty for good and retrained as a barista, and it
translates its old job into the new one without missing a beat: latency is
now steaming time, uptime is cafe hours, and the only tickets it closes
anymore sit on the pastry case. It signs off with the required pun --
"oat of office" -- which lands because everything before it has been
deadpan-professional, the way a real auto-reply would sound if it were
honest about where it went.

## The entry

> Out of office: I have resigned from your inbox to become a barista. My latency is now steaming time, my uptime is café hours, and the only tickets I close are on the pastry case. For urgent matters, I am oat of office until further notice.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
